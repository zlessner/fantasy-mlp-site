How scoring works

A player's fantasy points at a stop are the sum of four things, tallied across every match their MLP team plays.

+3
per game won
up to 2 a match — your doubles + a mixed

+3

+1
stop title

Most a player can score in one stop: 46 — at a 5-match stop like Orlando (winning all 10 games + a clean 5-match regulation sweep + the title). It's a hard ceiling, and it's been hit twice: Anna Leigh Waters and Noe Khlif, both New Jersey, at Chicago.
